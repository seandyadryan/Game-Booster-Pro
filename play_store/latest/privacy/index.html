Effective date: October 15, 2026

PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti ("we", "us", or "our") provides Game Tools. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its cache tools, network diagnostics, game launcher, device dashboard, and optional Android permissions.

Information processed on your device

  • Device status: battery level, battery temperature, screen refresh rate, and system memory information are read to support the dashboard and system functions. These readings are not sent to us.
  • Apps and cache sizes: the app reads the names, package identifiers, icons, and cache sizes of launchable apps that Android makes visible. Game discovery uses Android game metadata. Cache sizes for other apps are read only after you grant Usage Access.
  • Foreground app events: with Usage Access, the app checks which package is on screen to control visibility of the monitor bar for the game you selected. It does not read the contents of games, messages, calls, or other app screens.
  • Network diagnostics: connection type, probe timings, and failed-probe counts are processed locally and shown in the Network Latency panel. Results are held in memory for the current panel and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Preferences: the app may store settings needed to restore its Do Not Disturb request in private app storage. Android may include eligible preferences in system backup according to your device and account settings.

Network connections and third parties

When you tap Check now or Check again in Network Latency, the app makes five TCP connection attempts to Cloudflare's public endpoint at 1.1.1.1 on port 443. These attempts measure connection latency on the active network. They do not send your app list, cache information, game data, message content, or diagnostic report.

As with any Internet connection, the endpoint and network operators can receive connection metadata such as your public IP address and connection timing. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. We do not operate this endpoint and do not control its retention practices. The check is optional and runs only when you request it.

The app does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, user account system, or developer-operated data-upload service. We do not sell app data.

Android permissions and your choices

  • Internet and network state: used for user-requested latency checks and identifying the active network type. The app does not request access to your precise location or Wi-Fi network name.
  • Usage Access: optional access used to read other launchable apps' cache sizes and identify when the selected game is on screen. It does not grant permission to delete another app's cache automatically.
  • Display over other apps: optional access used to show a small monitor bar over the selected game. Tapping the bar removes it.
  • Do Not Disturb access: optional access used to request a strict interruption filter and end the app's request when the session ends. Strict Do Not Disturb can silence alarms and media; it does not reject calls or guarantee that call screens are hidden.

You may decline or revoke optional access in Android settings. Basic app-cache cleanup does not require Usage Access. Denying an optional permission disables the related function.

Cache cleanup and your files

Clear This App's Cache and Start Session remove temporary files only from Game Tools' internal, code-cache, and external app-cache directories. The app reports the bytes removed and the remaining cache. It does not delete your preferences, game saves, other apps' private data, or personal media. Cache files may be recreated during normal use. Cache cleanup for other apps is performed manually through Android settings.

Retention, deletion, and privacy requests

Device readings and diagnostic results are not stored on our servers. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restore settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you provided in a support message, contact our support email. We will handle applicable privacy requests according to the laws that apply to you and any necessary legal retention requirements.

Security and international processing

Local preferences use Android's app-private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party network endpoints and email providers may process connection metadata or support messages in countries different from yours. We do not upload locally read app lists, cache rankings, or foreground app events.

Children's privacy

Game Tools is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If a child has provided personal information through a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion, subject to applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or information practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app, its store listing, or this policy page as appropriate.
