# Artwork Generation Record

Mode: built-in imagegen tool. No CLI/API-key fallback was used. Source outputs were preserved in the Codex generated-images directory. The selected results were mechanically fitted to the upload dimensions using Sharp.

## Feature Graphic Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Generate one Google Play feature graphic for the Android utility app named exactly 'Game Tools: Network & Cache'. Landscape 1024x500 pixel composition or closest aspect 2.048:1. Clearly typeset the full app name on the left in large white professional sans-serif, optionally on two lines. Under it typeset exactly 'Network latency. App cache. Game launcher.' On the right a refined studio illustration of a game controller, a recognizable Wi-Fi symbol and small storage blocks, arranged as a compact utility toolkit. Charcoal and mint accents with touches of cyan and silver. Flat final promotional banner, crisp readable typography, generous safe margins. Convey practical game-adjacent utility, not performance acceleration. No speedometer, no rocket, no motion speed lines, no flames, no numeric performance readings, no FPS claims, no boost claims, no AI claims, no ranking or pricing or reviews, no stock people, no invented app screenshots. Only the exact text requested above.
