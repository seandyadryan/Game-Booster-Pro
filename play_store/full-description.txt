Game Booster Pro is an Android gaming companion that brings cache tools, network checks, device information, and game shortcuts into one dashboard.

START A GAMING SESSION
Tap Boost Now to clear Game Booster Pro's own temporary cache and see the size before cleanup, the bytes removed, and the remaining cache. The launch animation marks the start of your session. Dont Disturb is optional and requires your permission and confirmation.

AI NETWORK: CHECK YOUR CONNECTION
Run five TCP connection probes on your active Wi-Fi, mobile data, Ethernet, or VPN connection. See average, minimum, and maximum latency in milliseconds, jitter between consecutive successful probes, and the number of failed probes. Results are measured locally, not generated by AI. The check uses Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1:443 endpoint and measures connection latency. It is not an Internet speed test, game-server ping measurement, or network accelerator.

CACHE TOOLS WITH CLEAR RESULTS
View and clear this app's own temporary cache without deleting your preferences or game data. With Usage Access, view the five largest caches among launchable apps that Android makes visible. Open an app's Android settings to clear its cache manually. Usage Access does not authorize automatic deletion of other apps' caches. Cache ranking requires Android 8 or later.

YOUR GAMES, IN ONE PLACE
Find installed apps identified as games by Android and launch the selected package directly. The available list depends on the game's metadata and Android's app visibility rules.

DONT DISTURB CONTROLS
Request strict Dont Disturb to silence interruptions. The mode also silences alarms and media. Calls, including WhatsApp calls, may still arrive or appear on screen. When Game Booster Pro enables Dont Disturb for a session, it ends its own request when you disconnect; other Android rules may keep Dont Disturb active.

DISPLAY AND DEVICE INFORMATION
View screen refresh rate in Hz, battery percentage, and battery temperature where available. GFX Tools opens your device's display settings and game launcher. Graphics quality, resolution, and FPS limits inside a game remain controlled by the game.

OPTIONAL GAME MONITOR BAR
With Usage Access and Display over other apps permission, show a small refresh-rate monitor over the selected game. Android does not expose another game's actual FPS to ordinary apps. The bar shows screen Hz and marks FPS as unavailable. Refresh rate is not game FPS.

ANDROID APP MANAGEMENT
Open Android's app-management settings to review background apps. This app does not silently force-stop other apps or clear the Recent Apps screen.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Device information, cache rankings, and diagnostic results are processed on your device. No account, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK is included. Running AI Network contacts a third-party endpoint that can receive your public IP address. Optional system permissions can be revoked in Android settings.

Features depend on Android version, device settings, and permissions. Game Booster Pro does not guarantee higher FPS, reduced game-server ping, or improved game performance.
